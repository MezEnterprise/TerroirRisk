How to read this

The colors

Each field is colored by how it measured on the selected indicator (pick one of the four tabs when you open a field), for the selected year (bottom bar). Blue/green = healthier reading, red/brown = more stressed. The scale is fixed across all fields and all years, so a color means the same thing everywhere on this map.

The four indicators

Vigor (NDVI) — how dense and green the canopy is. The standard vegetation reading from satellite imagery.

Water status (NDMI) — how much water is in the canopy right now. Usually the first to react to a dry spell.

Chlorophyll (NDRE) — a more sensitive read on plant health, often moves before visible stress shows up.

Biomass (EVI) — canopy volume, less sensitive to soil color showing through than NDVI, useful for sparse or young vines.

Using the map

Click any field for its detail and 2017–2026 trend. Use the year bar at the bottom to see how the whole map changes across a decade. The 8 highlighted fields (opaque, listed on the left) are the ones used in the statistical analysis; the rest are shown for context.

Where this data comes from

Sentinel-2 satellite imagery (European Space Agency, Copernicus programme), 10 m resolution, 2017–2026. Field boundaries from SIGPAC (Spain's official agricultural parcel registry) and visual correction against recent imagery. Rainfall reference from AEMET, Spain's national weather service.
